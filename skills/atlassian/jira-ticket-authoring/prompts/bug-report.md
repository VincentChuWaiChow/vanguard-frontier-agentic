# Bug report

@jira-ticket-authoring

Draft a Jira bug report from the information below. Preserve the bug-report format even if the issue has not yet been reproduced.

Include:
- A title describing the observable failure.
- Reported or observed behaviour.
- Expected behaviour and the source of that expectation.
- Affected product, version, environment, and user scope where known.
- Supplied reproduction steps, reproduction status, and available evidence.
- Business or customer impact.
- Proposed investigation, regression checks, and unresolved questions.

Keep suspected causes separate from established findings. Distinguish severity, urgency, and priority.

Assess readiness for triage separately from readiness to implement a fix. Mark proposed checks as not performed.

Report and evidence:
[Paste the customer report, reproduction notes, timestamps, screenshots, sanitized logs, expected behaviour, and relevant links.]
