# Turn a product idea into a PRD and Jira backlog

@confluence-document-authoring @jira-ticket-authoring

Turn the information below into a coordinated product specification and proposed delivery backlog.

Produce:
1. A plain-language summary of the problem, affected users, and intended business outcome.
2. A Confluence PRD covering requirements, business rules, scope, exclusions, success measures, dependencies, and open decisions.
3. A proposed Jira epic and appropriately scoped child tickets, using the relevant templates.
4. A list of documentation and support changes needed for delivery.

Preserve traceability between PRD requirements and ticket acceptance criteria. Distinguish discovery work from implementation work, and identify the outcome and verification approach for each ticket.

Use local planning references until actual Jira keys are available. Assess each ticket’s readiness independently and identify dependencies that affect sequencing.

Return the complete drafts and the material questions that remain unresolved.

Input:
[Paste the product idea, customer feedback, business goals, current workflow, technical context, constraints, and relevant source material.]
