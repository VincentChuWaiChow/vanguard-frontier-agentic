# Customer support case

@jira-ticket-authoring

Draft a customer support case from the information below.

Summarize the customer’s problem and requested outcome in plain language. Record the affected product or account scope, timeline, business impact, troubleshooting already performed, and available evidence.

Separate customer statements, confirmed findings, hypotheses, and proposed next steps. Identify the smallest useful next investigation and any missing information needed to route the case.

Recommend an engineering handoff if the evidence supports one, explaining what that handoff should contain. Preserve existing commitments and service levels only where supplied.

Include a concise customer-response draft that explains the current understanding and asks for the necessary information.

State whether the case is ready for triage and what remains unresolved before resolution or closure.
