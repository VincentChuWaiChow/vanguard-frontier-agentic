# Plan an experiment and record results

@confluence-document-authoring

Draft a Confluence experiment plan and results document using the [experiment template](../assets/templates/experiment-plan-results.md).

Define the question, falsifiable hypothesis, comparison, population, primary metric, guardrails, sampling rationale, analysis method, and stopping conditions from the supplied information. Keep execution permission and applicable privacy/consent requirements explicit.

Leave results **NOT RUN** unless actual observations are supplied. Preserve protocol changes and deviations. Separate observations, interpretation, uncertainty, confounding, and next decisions; do not claim causation or a proved hypothesis without appropriate evidence.

Return the complete draft, using **Unknown** for missing facts. Do not launch the experiment, publish, or authorize rollout.
