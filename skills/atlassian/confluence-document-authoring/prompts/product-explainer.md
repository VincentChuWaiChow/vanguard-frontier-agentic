# Explain a product in business terms

@confluence-document-authoring

Draft a Confluence product explainer for [target audience: finance, operations, management, sales, or another business team].

Use plain language and explain:
- What the product does.
- The problem it addresses and who benefits.
- How it fits into the user’s everyday workflow.
- Its main capabilities and important limitations.
- The business meaning of essential technical terms.
- How users can get started or obtain help.

Distinguish intended benefits from measured results. Support claims about savings, performance, availability, and eligibility with the supplied evidence.

Include a short workflow example grounded in the source material and a concise FAQ. Place implementation detail in an optional technical section.
