# Create a support knowledge-base article

@confluence-document-authoring

Draft a Confluence support knowledge-base article for [customers or internal support staff] using the information below.

Start with the symptom or question readers will recognize. Identify the affected product, versions, environments, and conditions where known.

Structure the article around:
- Symptoms and business impact.
- Confirmed findings and unresolved hypotheses.
- Prerequisites for troubleshooting.
- Safe checks, their expected observations, and what to do if results differ.
- Validated workarounds or resolution steps.
- Stopping conditions and escalation information.
- Evidence needed to confirm resolution.

Clearly distinguish validated procedures from proposed investigation steps. Keep information appropriate for the intended audience.

Source material:
[Paste the support case, error messages, troubleshooting results, known product behaviour, verified workaround or fix, and escalation details.]
