# Draft a team working agreement

@confluence-document-authoring

Draft a Confluence team purpose and working agreement for [team and audience] using the [working-agreement template](../assets/templates/team-working-agreement.md).

Explain the team's purpose, beneficiaries, scope boundaries, responsibilities, partner interfaces, collaboration practices, decision process, and review triggers. Link existing policies and team discussion evidence where available.

Keep proposed practices separate from confirmed agreements. Do not invent response-time promises, employment terms, access permissions, or organizational policy. Use relevant roles rather than collecting unnecessary personal details. Distinguish intended benefits from measured team outcomes.

Return the complete draft, open questions, and **Unknown** for missing facts. Do not publish or imply consent from unconfirmed participants.
