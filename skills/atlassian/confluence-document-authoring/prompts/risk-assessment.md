# Assess initiative risks

@confluence-document-authoring

Draft a Confluence risk assessment for [bounded initiative] using the [risk assessment template](../assets/templates/risk-assessment.md).

Describe each cause, risk event, consequence, evidence, owner, and uncertainty. Use the organization's supplied likelihood/impact definitions; label any suggested scale proposed rather than an established standard.

Separate existing controls from proposed treatments, and documented controls from verified effectiveness. Include validation checks and escalation triggers. Do not invent numeric probabilities, imply regulatory certification, or mark residual risk accepted without actual authority and decision evidence.

Return the complete draft and material unknowns, using **Unknown** where needed. Do not execute mitigations or publish the assessment.
