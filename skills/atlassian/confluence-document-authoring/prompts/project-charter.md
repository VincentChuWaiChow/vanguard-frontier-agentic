# Draft a project charter and approval gates

@confluence-document-authoring

Draft a Confluence project charter for [initiative and audience] using the [charter template](../assets/templates/project-charter.md).

Explain the evidenced problem, intended value, alternatives, proposed mandate, scope, stakeholder decision rights, resource estimates, risks, and unresolved authorization gates. Keep detailed delivery sequencing in a linked project plan rather than duplicating it.

Separate proposed sponsor and allocations from confirmed authority and actual funding decisions. Do not invent budget, currency, staffing, a contingency percentage, approved dates, or agreement. Expected benefits remain unmeasured unless results are supplied.

Return the complete draft, specific decisions needed, and **Unknown** for missing facts. Do not publish, approve expenditure, assign people, procure services, or execute the initiative.
