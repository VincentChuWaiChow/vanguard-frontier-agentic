# Draft a product roadmap

@confluence-document-authoring

Draft a Confluence product roadmap for [product, audience, and planning horizon] using the [roadmap template](../assets/templates/product-roadmap.md).

Organize initiatives around user and business outcomes, priority rationale, dependencies, capacity constraints, and confidence. Use existing horizon names where available; label a new structure proposed.

Separate directional plans, forecast dates, and actual commitments with their sources and authority. Do not turn a roadmap into a delivery promise or infer customer availability from merged or deployed work. Distinguish expected benefits from measured outcomes.

Return the complete draft with open decisions and **Unknown** for missing evidence. Do not publish it or create delivery commitments.
