# Assess a change's customer impact

@confluence-document-authoring

Draft a Confluence customer-impact assessment for [proposed change and audience] using the [customer-impact template](../assets/templates/customer-impact-assessment.md).

Explain the current and proposed workflows, affected cohorts, anticipated impact, timing, dependencies, and evidence limits. Include mitigation proposals, support readiness, communications drafts, change gates, and post-change measurement needs.

Distinguish expected effects from observed customer results. Preserve only supplied service commitments; do not invent customer counts, SLAs, risk tiers, or a disruption-free guarantee. Use aggregate cohort information and avoid unnecessary personal data.

Return the complete draft with **Unknown** for missing evidence. Assessment readiness does not authorize a production change, publication, or sending customer communications.

Change inputs:
[Paste the change proposal, affected workflows, customer evidence, existing commitments, support needs, approvals, recovery references, and relevant links.]
