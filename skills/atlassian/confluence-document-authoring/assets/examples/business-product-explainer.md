## What it would do

Service Request Hub is a proposed shared place for employees to submit service requests and see their progress. Reviewers would use the same request record to understand the need, ask for details, and record an outcome. These are intended behaviors in the synthetic proposal, not verified product capabilities. [S1](#s1-synthetic-product-proposal)

## Who it would help and why

The intended users are employees requesting an internal service and the people who review those requests. The synthetic process note reports that requests arrive by email and reviewers keep a separate tracking list. It also reports that requesters ask reviewers for updates.
